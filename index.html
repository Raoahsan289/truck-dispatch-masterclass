Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior logistics experience or a specific degree?

No prior experience or logistics degree is required! The Master Class is built from the ground up for total beginners. We cover US freight terminology, load board mechanics, and broker scripts step by step.

What hardware and equipment do I need to start?

All you need is a basic computer or laptop, a reliable internet connection, and a smartphone or VoIP phone service (such as Google Voice or OpenPhone) to communicate with freight brokers.

Can I work as a US freight dispatcher remotely from outside the US?

Yes! US freight dispatching is a thriving global remote career. As long as you can speak English clearly and operate during US business hours (EST/CST/PST), you can dispatch trucks remotely from anywhere.

How does the Certificate Verification work?

Every graduate receives an official Certificate issued under RouteCraft Logistics Corp. with a unique ID format (RC-TDMC-XXXXXX). Anyone can verify your credentials on our live Verification Portal.
