MODULE 08 • FINANCIALS & BILLING

Invoicing, Factoring & Billing Management 💳

Master the post-delivery billing workflow. Learn how to prepare clean invoice packets, submit documents to factoring companies, collect dispatcher service fees, and manage carrier cash flow.

1. The Post-Delivery Billing Package

Once a load is successfully delivered, a dispatcher must quickly assemble the complete Billing Packet to get the carrier paid. Any missing document or blurry delivery signature delays payment processing.

Required Document Source / Purpose Dispatcher Billing Audit
Clean Bill of Lading (BOL/POD) Signed by receiver at unloading dock Must show clear receiver signature, date, and zero damage notes.
Signed Rate Confirmation Agreed broker contract Verify linehaul rate and any approved lumper/detention fees match.
Carrier Invoice Issued by carrier or dispatcher software Itemized breakdown of base rate, fuel surcharge, and accessorials.
Lumper / Fuel Receipts Out-of-pocket delivery expenses Attach original legible receipts for full broker reimbursement.

2. Payment Methods & Factoring Mechanics

Freight Factoring Same-Day Cash

Factoring companies buy carrier invoices at a small discount (1%–3%) to pay the carrier within 24 hours instead of waiting 30–60 days.

Payout Window Same-day or 24 hours
Factoring Fee 1.5% – 3.5% of gross invoice
Direct Broker Billing Standard Terms

Submitting billing directly to broker accounting. Payment is processed according to broker payment terms (Net 30).

Standard Terms Net 21 to Net 30 Days
Quick Pay Option 1–3 Days (Broker charges 2%–5%)
Dispatcher Commission Your Revenue

Collecting your dispatch service percentage (typically 5%–10% of gross load revenue) weekly from your carrier clients.

Standard Rate 5% – 10% of gross gross revenue
Invoicing Cycle Weekly or bi-weekly billing
💡 Pro Dispatcher Rule: Immediate Invoicing Yields Fast Pay

Train your drivers to snap high-resolution, flat photos of signed BOLs at the receiver dock before pulling away. Uploading PODs into the factoring portal or emailing brokers within 1 hour of delivery keeps carrier cash flow healthy!
