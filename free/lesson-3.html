Trailer Specifications & Equipment Types 🚛

Master standard trailer dimensions, legal weight limits, temperature requirements, and specialized equipment categories. Knowing these precise specifications is critical to avoid illegal overload fines and dispatching errors.

1. Legal Gross Weight & Federal Regulations (FMCSA / DOT)

In the United States, the federal gross vehicle weight limit for an 18-wheeler without special permits is 80,000 lbs. As a dispatcher, you must calculate the total weight including the tractor, empty trailer, fuel, and payload.

Weight Category Maximum Legal Allowance Dispatcher Operational Note
Gross Vehicle Weight (GVW) 80,000 lbs Combined weight of truck, trailer, driver, fuel, and cargo.
Steer Axle Limit 12,000 lbs Front tires of the tractor.
Drive Axles Limit 34,000 lbs Tandem axles directly behind truck cab.
Trailer Axles Limit 34,000 lbs Tandem axles at the rear of the trailer.

2. Core Trailer Categories

Dry Van (DV) Most Common

Enclosed box trailer used to transport non-perishable palletized goods, electronics, clothing, and dry food products.

Standard Length 53 Feet
Dimensions (W x H) 102 in x 110 in
Max Freight Weight 42,000 – 45,000 lbs
Pallet Capacity 26 Standard Pallets (48x40)
Refrigerated (Reefer) Temp-Controlled

Insulated box trailer with a heavy-duty cooling unit (Reefer Unit) attached to transport fresh produce, pharmaceuticals, and frozen foods.

Standard Length 53 Feet
Temperature Range -20°F to +70°F
Max Freight Weight 42,000 – 44,000 lbs (Heavier)
Pallet Capacity 26 Standard Pallets
Flatbed (FB) Open Deck

Open platform trailer without sides or roof. Loaded from the top, sides, or rear using forklifts or cranes for construction material and steel.

Standard Length 48 Ft or 53 Ft
Max Legal Height 8 Ft 6 in Cargo Height
Max Freight Weight 48,000 lbs
Securment Straps, Chains, Tarps
💡 Pro Dispatcher Rule: Weight & Reefer Unit Mechanics

Reefer trailers carry a refrigeration unit (e.g., Thermo King / Carrier) and an extra fuel tank beneath the floor. This extra gear adds approximately 1,500 to 2,000 lbs of empty dead weight compared to a standard Dry Van. Always confirm the carrier's exact empty scale weight before booking 45,000 lb heavy loads!

3. Specialized Open Deck Equipment

  • Step Deck (Single Drop): Designed with a lower deck floor to transport tall equipment (up to 10 feet high) without requiring special oversize permits.
  • RGN (Removable Gooseneck): The front of the trailer detaches, allowing heavy wheeled machinery (excavators, bulldozers) to drive directly onto the trailer deck.
  • Conestoga: A flatbed trailer equipped with a retractable tarping system on a rolling frame. Protects sensitive machinery while allowing side loading.
