MODULE 05 • CARRIER ONBOARDING

Carrier Onboarding & The Legal Carrier Packet 📄

Learn how to onboard new trucking clients, assemble mandatory legal carrier packets (MC Authority, COI, W-9, NOA), and establish Dispatcher-Carrier Agreements to legally represent motor carriers.

1. The Four Pillar Documents of a Carrier Packet

Before a freight broker can legally assign a load to a carrier, the dispatcher must submit a completed Carrier Setup Packet. Missing or outdated documents will halt loading process immediately.

Document Name Issuing Authority / Purpose Dispatcher Audit Checkpoint
MC Authority (Form MCSA-1) FMCSA Operating Authority Certificate Verify Active status on FMCSA SAFER website and authority age.
Certificate of Insurance (COI) Insurance Broker / Underwriter Must list $1,000,000 Auto Liability & $100,000 Cargo Coverage minimum.
Form W-9 IRS Tax Identification Document Must be signed, dated, and match the legal entity on MC Authority.
Notice of Assignment (NOA) Factoring Company Directs broker remittance to the carrier's factoring bank account.

2. Key Setup Documents Explained

MC Authority & DOT FMCSA Compliance

The federal license granting motor carriers permission to haul interstate commerce.

Authority Type Motor Carrier (Property)
Verification SAFER Web System
Broker Age Requirement 30 to 90 days active minimum
Insurance (COI) Risk Shield

Protects cargo, vehicles, and third parties against freight damage or road accidents.

Auto Liability $1,000,000 Minimum
Cargo Coverage $100,000 Minimum
Certificate Holder Broker added before dispatch
Factoring & NOA Cash Flow

Ensures carriers get paid within 24 hours of delivering proof of delivery (BOL).

NOA Requirement Mandatory for factored trucks
Payment Address Must match factoring bank
