Rate Confirmations & Dispatch Workflows 📝

Learn how to carefully audit Rate Confirmations (Rate Cons), issue driver dispatch instructions, manage pick/drop appointments, and monitor real-time tracking updates across active transit.

1. What is a Rate Confirmation (Rate Con)?

A Rate Confirmation is a legally binding contract issued by a freight broker to a motor carrier once a load is agreed upon. It details the exact rate, pickup/delivery locations, timing, cargo weight, and accessorial terms.

Critical Section Key Information Included Dispatcher Audit Checkpoint
Agreed Gross Pay Total linehaul amount, fuel surcharge, and approved accessorials. Confirm the dollar amount matches your negotiated telephone offer exactly.
Addresses & Times Shipper & Consignee facility details and strict appointment windows. Verify pickup/delivery dates line up with driver's Hours of Service (HOS).
Special Instructions Temperature settings, tarping rules, strap counts, and dock check-ins. Failure to comply with fine prints can forfeit detention or void payment.

💡 Pro Dispatcher Rule: Protect Your Detention Pay

If a driver is held at a loading/unloading facility longer than 2 hours (free time), you must notify the broker in writing immediately! Ensure the driver gets their arrival and departure times stamped on the Bill of Lading (BOL) to secure hourly detention compensation ($50–$75/hr).

3. Common Accessorial Fees & Claims Prevention

  • TONU (Truck Ordered Not Used): Issued when a broker cancels a load after the driver has already dispatched to the pickup location. Standard TONU rates range from $150 to $250.
  • Lumper Fees: Third-party labor costs at grocery or retail warehouses for unloading freight. Dispatchers must request lumper receipts and advance payment approval from the broker.
