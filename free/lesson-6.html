MODULE 06 • RATE NEGOTIATION

Rate Negotiation & Broker Phone Scripts 📞

Master professional phone etiquette, counter-offer psychology, market leverage identification, and proven scripts to maximize rate-per-mile (RPM) when dealing with freight brokers.

1. The Psychology of Broker Negotiations

Brokers aim to secure the lowest possible cost for their shipper clients, while dispatchers fight for the highest profit for their truck drivers. Successful negotiation relies on data, timing, and project confidence—never on emotion or arguing.

Negotiation Factor Broker's Objective Dispatcher's Counter Strategy
Initial Posted Rate Leave room for downward pressure or quick baseline bookings. Treat the posted rate as an opening offer, not a final ceiling.
Market Urgency Cover loads quickly before shipping deadlines expire. Leverage tight truck-to-load ratios in high-demand outbound markets.
Driver Quality Ensure safe, reliable, on-time delivery without claims. Highlight elite carrier safety scores, clean history, and immediate availability.

💡 Pro Dispatcher Rule: The Counter-Offer Formula

Never ask: "Is that the best rate you can do?" Instead, use data-backed positioning: "Based on current outbound capacity in this market and lane averages, our truck needs $X,XXX to make this work. Can we get that approved?"

3. Handling Common Broker Objections

  • "That's all the shipper is paying us": Politely remind the broker that your job is to secure fair market value for your carrier's operating expenses and fuel costs. Offer to look at alternative loads if the margin doesn't align.
  • "We have other trucks willing to take it for less": If true, hold your ground or move to the next load. Often, this is a bluff to force a lower price. Stand firm on your lane analysis.
