Load Boards, Trailer Specs & Rate-Per-Mile Math 🚚

Master trailer capacities, load board mechanics, and rate calculation formulas to secure profitable freight.

1. Key Trailer Types & Freight Specifications

Before booking any load on a load board, you must understand the three primary equipment types in the US trucking industry:

Equipment Type Standard Length Max Freight Weight Common Cargo
Dry Van (DV) 53 Feet 42,000 – 45,000 lbs Boxed goods, electronics, non-perishable food, retail items.
Reefer (Refrigerated) 53 Feet 42,000 – 44,000 lbs Fresh produce, frozen meats, pharmaceuticals, floral goods.
Flatbed (FB) 48 – 53 Feet 48,000 lbs Lumber, steel coils, heavy machinery, building materials.

2. Rate-Per-Mile (RPM) Math Formula

As a dispatcher, your main job is ensuring every trip yields a solid profit margin for your carrier. Use these fundamental formulas:

Formula 1: Rate Per Mile (RPM) = Total Broker Gross Payout ÷ Total Trip Miles

Formula 2: Net Carrier Pay = Total Payout - Dispatcher Fee (e.g., 8%) - Estimated Fuel Cost
