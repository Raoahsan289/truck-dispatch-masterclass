Introduction to Load Boards (DAT & Truckstop) 💻

Learn how professional freight dispatchers navigate primary load boards like DAT One and Truckstop.com, filter active freight searches, understand headhauls versus backhauls, and identify profitable lanes.

Marketplace Core

1. The Big Two: DAT One vs. Truckstop.com

📊

Load boards are online marketplaces where freight brokers post available loads for carriers, and carriers or dispatchers search for matching equipment. Mastering these platforms is the core daily duty of a freight dispatcher.

Load Board Platform Key Strengths Dispatcher Operational Focus
DAT One (Dial-A-Truck) Largest freight network, spot market rate history tools, and quick credit scores. Best for checking historical lane rates and finding nationwide dry van/reefer loads.
Truckstop.com Load alerts, rate per mile insights (RateMate), and integrated factoring checks. Great for flatbed freight, specialized lanes, and fast broker contact access.

💡 Pro Dispatcher Rule: Speed Wins the Load

Good loads on DAT One or Truckstop are booked within minutes of posting. Keep your dispatcher call script ready, check broker credit scores instantly, and call the broker immediately instead of just emailing when you find a lucrative lane!

3. Evaluating Broker Credit & Days to Pay

  • DAT Score / Ansonia Credit: Always check the broker's credit score and average Days-to-Pay (DTP). Avoid dispatching for brokers with scores under 90 or DTP exceeding 45+ days unless using a factoring company.
  • Posted Rate vs. Negotiable Rate: Most load board rates are initial offers. Professional dispatchers use spot rate tools to evaluate the true lane average before calling the broker to negotiate a higher rate.
